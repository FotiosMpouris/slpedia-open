# Contributing to SLP'edia

Thanks for helping. SLP'edia grows through suggestions from SLPs, researchers, educators, families and AAC users.

## Suggest a tool or study

The easiest way is email. Write to [editor@slpedia.world](mailto:editor@slpedia.world?subject=Suggestion%20for%20SLP%27edia) with the name, a link and a line or two on why it belongs. A tool, article, study or person worth adding are all welcome. You don't need an account anywhere.

Comfortable with GitHub? You can also [open a "Suggest a tool or study" issue](https://github.com/FotiosMpouris/slpedia-open/issues/new/choose) and fill in the form. Please check the [directory data](data.json) and the [open issues](https://github.com/FotiosMpouris/slpedia-open/issues) first so it isn't a repeat.

Every suggestion is checked against our editorial standards before it goes up. Approved entries are added to the directory and the wiki during the weekly refresh. Suggestions that don't make it get a short reason.

## The bar

Like Future Tools, SLP'edia turns down more than it accepts. A suggestion should be:

- **Real.** A working product, a published study or an active organization. No concepts, mockups or announcements.
- **Usable now.** Publicly available today, with working download, store or access links. No waitlists, private betas or "coming soon" pages.
- **Relevant to speech-language pathology.** Useful to SLPs or the people they work with: AAC, language, speech, fluency, voice, swallowing, literacy, assessment and related areas.
- **Honest about evidence.** Say what evidence exists: a systematic review, a trial, a single-case study, a position statement, user reviews, or none yet. Tools don't need a trial, but claims should be backed up. For research, link a real journal article, conference paper or preprint, and an open-access copy if there is one.
- **Not a brochure.** No placeholder testimonials, no unsupported outcome claims, and no pages that exist only to sell.
- **Not a duplicate.** If it's already listed, suggest a correction to the existing card instead.

Makers are welcome to suggest their own tools. Please say so in your email or issue. Listings are never paid, and there are no affiliate links.

## Corrections

Spotted a broken link, an outdated price, a removed app or a factual mistake? Email editor@slpedia.world or open an issue, name the card and include a source for the fix. Pull requests that edit `data.json` or the `wiki/` pages are welcome for small fixes.

## Please don't

- Include patient or client information, or anything that could identify one.
- Paste copyrighted full text from papers. A link and a summary in your own words are enough.
