### Nothing about AAC users without AAC users (Blasko, Light, McNaughton, Williams & Zimmerman, 2025)

- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1080/07434618.2025.2514748
- **People / org:** Grant Blasko, Janice Light, David McNaughton, Bob Williams, Jordyn Zimmerman
- **Region:** United States; Augmentative and Alternative Communication
- **Desk filters:** Research, People, Broader SLP
- **Summary:** Lead paper from the Future of AAC Research special issue: argues AAC users must lead and co-create research, tech, and training priorities, not be token add-ons. Names risks (tokenism, ableism) and the practical shift toward mutual-respect collaboration under “nothing about us without us.”

### PaiCoach: AI-supported parent coaching for autistic children (Akemoğlu et al., 2026)

- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s10803-026-07519-6
- **People / org:** Yusuf Akemoğlu, Zhaohui Li, Qingxiao Zheng, Emily Brooke Roberts, Samar Pratap Singh et al.
- **Region:** Turkey / international; Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders
- **Desk filters:** Research, Autism & kids, People
- **Summary:** Single-case study of Parent’s AI Coach (PaiCoach): human-reviewed AI that scores parent–child shared-reading videos and returns expert-validated feedback on modeling, mand-model, and time-delay strategies. Early lane for AI parent coaching, complementary to (not a replacement for) AAC devices.

### School professionals’ knowledge of autistic speech and AAC decisions (Zisk et al., 2024)

- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0044-1793928
- **People / org:** Alyssa Hillary Zisk, Brandon Eddy, Amy L. Donaldson, Sara Cannalonga, Olivia Strickland et al.
- **Region:** United States; Seminars in Speech and Language
- **Desk filters:** Research, Autism & kids, People
- **Summary:** Participatory survey of 567 school-based professionals on autistic speech and AAC decision-making, co-led with autistic AAC users. Highlights knowledge gaps that drive under- or mis-prescription of AAC when speech is present but unreliable, and pushes for better training on intermittent/speech+AAC profiles.
