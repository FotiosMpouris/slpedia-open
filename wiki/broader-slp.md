### AAC supports in a community museum for young children (Yacucci & Ballard, 2026)

- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1080/07434618.2025.2604825
- **People / org:** Amy L. Yacucci, Sarah L. Ballard
- **Region:** United States; Augmentative and Alternative Communication
- **Desk filters:** Research, Autism & kids, Broader SLP
- **Summary:** Utilization-focused evaluation of multimodal AAC supports in a children’s museum (majority of represented children autistic). Found gains in communication access and social awareness, plus a clear need for ongoing staff training, customization, and multiple accessible formats: AAC beyond the therapy room.

### AAC use in South African classrooms: challenges and implications (Terblanche, Pascoe & Harty, 2025)

- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1177/02656590241311063
- **People / org:** Camryn Terblanche, Michelle Pascoe, Michal Harty
- **Region:** South Africa; Child Language Teaching and Therapy
- **Desk filters:** Research, Broader SLP
- **Summary:** Exploratory focus-group study of AAC in South African classrooms: affordability, device security/crime risk, training gaps, multilingual realities, and implementation load shape whether AAC actually gets used. Essential Global South framing for feature-match thinking that assumes US/UK infrastructure.

### External challenges for AAC language learning (Holyfield et al., 2025)

- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1080/07434618.2025.2508485

- **Region:** United States; Augmentative and Alternative Communication
- **Desk filters:** Research, Autism & kids, Broader SLP

### FIATS-AAC (family impact / AAC outcome measure)

- **URL:** https://hollandbloorview.ca/research-education/bloorview-research-institute/outcome-measures/AAC-measures/FIATS-AAC
- **People / org:** Holland Bloorview Kids Rehabilitation Hospital (measure origin); Norwegian adaptation: Fjeldvang, Nordaas, von Tetzchner, Stadskleiv
- **Region:** Canada origin; Norwegian clinical adaptation published 2023
- **Desk filters:** Research, Broader SLP
- **Summary:** Parent-report outcome measure capturing how AAC interventions affect child participation and family life (ages ~3–18). Norwegian short-form validation (FIATS-AAC-No) found acceptable reliability/validity and clinician-rated usefulness for follow-up. It moves AAC evaluation beyond “device delivered.”

### ISAAC Position Statement on Facilitated Communication

- **People / org:** International Society for Augmentative and Alternative Communication (ISAAC)
- **Region:** International (ISAAC)
- **Desk filters:** Autism & kids, Broader SLP, Research
