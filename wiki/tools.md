### Avaz AAC

- **URL:** https://www.avazapp.com/
- **People / org:** Avaz Inc.
- **Region:** India (used internationally)
- **Desk filters:** Tools, Autism & kids
- **Summary:** Picture- and text-based speech-generating app for children with speech/language needs, widely used with autistic kids. Built for home–school–therapist teams; iOS and Android; often positioned as a more affordable full-featured AAC option. Feature-match like any SGD.

### Cboard AAC (open-source)

- **URL:** https://www.cboard.io/
- **People / org:** Cboard open-source project / cboard-org
- **Region:** International (open-source; UNICEF Innovation–linked distribution)
- **Desk filters:** Tools, Autism & kids, Broader SLP
- **Summary:** Free, open-source symbol + text-to-speech AAC web/app supporting many languages, custom boards, and offline use on Chromium. Useful equity option when proprietary AAC is inaccessible; still requires SLP feature-matching and partner modelling like any SGD.

### CoughDrop

- **URL:** https://www.coughdrop.com/
- **People / org:** CoughDrop / Forbes AAC
- **Region:** United States (web / cross-platform)
- **Desk filters:** Tools, Autism & kids, Broader SLP
- **Summary:** Cloud-based AAC platform for people with complex communication needs: boards that sync across web, iOS, Android, Kindle, and Windows so home/school/clinic teams share the same system. Historically open-source; now commercially supported (Forbes AAC) with team modelling and reporting features.

### Fluent AAC

- **URL:** https://www.fluentaac.com/
- **People / org:** Fluent AAC
- **Region:** United States
- **Desk filters:** Tools, Autism & kids
- **Summary:** iPad AAC communication app for children and adults who have difficulty speaking, including autism, Down syndrome, apraxia, ALS, and stroke. Emphasizes a clean, modern interface and customizable vocabularies for everyday expression.

### Grid by Smartbox

- **URL:** https://thinksmartbox.com/
- **People / org:** Smartbox Assistive Technology
- **Region:** United Kingdom (used internationally)
- **Desk filters:** Tools, Autism & kids, Broader SLP
- **Summary:** Flagship AAC software from Smartbox (Grid 3 on Windows; Grid for iPad) for symbol and text communication, with eye gaze, switches, touch, and other access methods plus ready vocabularies (including PODD). Full SGD ecosystem for clinics that want a non–Tobii-Dynavox stack.

### LAMP Words for Life

- **URL:** https://lampwflapp.com/
- **People / org:** PRC-Saltillo; Center for AAC & Autism (LAMP approach)
- **Region:** United States (used internationally)
- **Desk filters:** Tools, Autism & kids
- **Summary:** Robust AAC vocabulary app built around Language Acquisition through Motor Planning (LAMP): consistent motor plans for words so language can grow without relearning paths. Especially common with autistic and other developmental communicators; iOS and PRC-Saltillo devices.

### Proloquo2Go

- **People / org:** AssistiveWare
- **Region:** International (AssistiveWare, Netherlands)

### TalkTablet PRO

### FreeVoice AAC

- **URL:** https://freevoiceaac.app/
- **People / org:** FreeVoice AAC / Chuea81
- **Region:** Global; open-source AAC (PWA + Android)
- **Desk filters:** Tools, Autism & kids
- **Summary:** Parent-built, MIT open-source symbol AAC (PWA + Android APK) with offline use after first load, no account, subscription, or tracking, ARASAAC symbols, a core-word bar, multilingual support including Arabic RTL, and on-device Kokoro TTS. A real free builder families can try today; customization depth is still maturing versus established paid apps.

### Pictalk AAC

- **URL:** https://www.pictalk.org/
- **People / org:** Association Pictalk
- **Region:** France / global; nonprofit AAC app
- **Desk filters:** Tools, Autism & kids, Broader SLP
- **Summary:** Nonprofit, pay-what-you-want symbol AAC with the ARASAAC library (about 40,000 pictograms), sentence bar, offline use, optional cloud sync, an 11-language interface, and text-to-speech across 30+ languages. A credible European alternative to subscription AAC with no ads or locked features; cloud sync requires an account.
