# The directory page

The directory is a single searchable page of cards titled **SLP'edia**. It is built from two files at the root of this repo: `index.html` (the page) and `data.json` (the cards).

- **Current live version:** [https://regal-inlet-xrbz.here.now/](https://regal-inlet-xrbz.here.now/)
- **Planned home:** slpedia.world (coming soon)
- **Run it yourself:** see "How to browse" in the [README](../README.md).

## What you see
A search box, filter chips and a feed of cards. Each card carries a title, summary, region, people or organization, and a link to the primary source.

## Filters (what they mean)

| Filter key | Label | Meaning |
|------------|-------|---------|
| `all` | All | Everything in the directory |
| `people` | People | Researchers, clinicians, advocacy and practice hubs with a named person or organization behind them |
| `autism` | Autism & kids | Autistic children and pediatric autism-related SLP |
| `tools` | Tools | **AAC tech only** (apps, software, SGD platforms) |
| `research` | Research | Papers, clearinghouses, labs and research agendas |
| `broader` | Broader SLP | Lifespan AAC, dysphagia standards, AT networks and adjacent SLP areas |

Cards often carry more than one filter key (for example, a paper tagged Research and Autism & kids).
