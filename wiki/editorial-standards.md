# Editorial standards

How SLP'edia entries are chosen, written and kept up to date.

## Truth over completeness
- Prefer verified, useful, clinician-facing facts over filling pages.
- If something is uncertain, say so. Never invent citations, URLs, product claims or outcomes.
- Prefer primary sources (papers, official organization pages, professional body guidance) over listicles.
- Link the real primary URL, not a mirror, whenever possible. When a paper is paywalled, add an open-access copy or abstract if one exists.

## Tools = tech only
- The **Tools** filter is reserved for AAC technology: apps, software, speech-generating device (SGD) platforms and cloud AAC systems.
- Practice portals, training modules, manuals and blogs don't go in Tools. They belong in People, Research, Autism & kids or Broader SLP.
- A tool has to be real and usable today: publicly available (no waitlists), working store or download links, and no placeholder testimonials.

## Global sources
- Aim for a global mix (UK and Ireland, the Nordics, the Netherlands, Germany, Japan, India, Australia, ISAAC and more) alongside strong US research.
- Non-English primary pages are welcome. Summaries are written in plain English and keep the real primary URL.

## English titles
- Card and wiki titles stay in English for searchability, even when the source is in another language.

## Adding and updating entries
1. New candidates are gathered each week, including suggestions from readers (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md)).
2. A person reviews every candidate against the bar above before it goes on the directory.
3. Approved cards are added to `data.json`, and the matching wiki page and [changelog](changelog.md) are updated.
4. A light check for broken links, duplicates and contradictions follows. Problems are fixed or flagged.
5. A new, distinct tool, person, hub or paper gets its own entry. New evidence about an existing entry updates that entry, with a note in the changelog.
6. The wiki can go deeper than a card, but it shouldn't contradict verified card facts without a source.

## Privacy
- No patient, client or personal health information, ever.
- No private contact details, and no outreach to the people or organizations listed unless they ask.

## Look and feel
- The directory page uses a high-contrast palette: a deep page background, cream cards, thick accent borders and yellow selected filters and tags.
- The wiki itself is plain markdown.
