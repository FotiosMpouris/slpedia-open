# About SLP'edia

**SLP'edia** is a free, open directory and newsletter that organizes the future of speech-language pathology: tools, technology and research studies. Think of it as "Future Tools, but for SLPs."

## Purpose
- Give speech-language pathologists a calm, high-signal directory they can open tomorrow morning.
- Prefer clinic-usable sources (feature-match AAC, partner modelling, evidence-based practice clearinghouses, outcome-oriented papers) over SEO listicles or unsupported AI hype.
- Grow a little every week through a human-reviewed refresh, so the directory stays current without turning into a dump.

## Audience
- **Primary:** speech-language pathologists, SLP students, educators and families interested in AAC and communication, with a strong lane for autistic children and pediatric autism-related SLP (AAC, language and partner coaching).
- **Secondary:** a broader SLP lane (lifespan AAC, dysphagia diet standards, related assistive technology networks), which will keep growing.

## What SLP'edia is not
- Not a clinical service. SLP'edia offers no therapy, assessments, mentoring, coaching, training or other services, and it doesn't sell anything.
- Not medical advice. Cards point to sources; clinical decisions stay with qualified professionals and the people they work with.
- Not a patient portal, and it holds no patient or client information.
- Not a dump of every AAC brand brochure. Tools means verified tech only.

## Not affiliated
SLP'edia is a free, open directory of SLP tools, technology and research. It isn't affiliated with SLPedia (slpedia.com), the speech-language practice in Long Beach, CA.
